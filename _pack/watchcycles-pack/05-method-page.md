# The method

Time = 1,064 / 364.
Price = scenario.
Not financial advice.

Watch Cycles is a calendar. It counts days between major cycle turns. It does not print a dollar.

## The clock

Three mature Bitcoin cycles rhymed on time:

| Cycle | Up (low → high) | Down (high → low) | Drawdown |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 1,068 days | 363 days | about −84% |
| C2 | 1,061 days | 376 days | about −77% |
| C3 | 1,050 days into Oct 6, 2025 | in progress | about −39% so far |

We treat that as:

- UP ≈ 1,064 days
- DOWN ≈ 364 days

C3 high used on this site: **Oct 6, 2025**, intraday near **$126,198**.
364 days after that date is **Oct 5, 2026**.

That date is the test. Not a promise.

## What this is

- A day-count overlay from prior major lows and highs
- A public date that can be wrong
- A live reading of how much of the down-leg is spent versus how much price has given back

## What this is not

- A trading signal
- A guarantee that price must visit any zone
- A claim that Bitcoin is governed by a timer
- Financial advice

Past rhyme can fail. Which high and low you pick changes the count. Intraday vs close changes the count. That is why the date is the product: it can be checked.

## Price boxes

Any dollar range on the site is a **historical analog**, not a forecast engine.

Prior down-legs cut much deeper than C3 has so far. If someone wants a memory aid, the last analog cluster sat far below the current price. That is context. The clock does not produce that number.

If Oct 5 arrives and price has not made a major low, we do not slide the analog around until it fits. We retire the map.

## What would falsify it

The old map is retired if **Oct 5, 2026** passes without a major cycle low in the window around that date.

We will write that result on the date. Hit or miss.

A later low can still happen. It just would not be this clock.

## How to read the homepage

- **Phase** — UP or DOWN, and which cycle
- **Days** — elapsed / 364 (or 1,064)
- **Vs high** — percent off the C3 high
- **To the test** — days to Oct 5, 2026
- **The tension line** — when time and price disagree

When time is almost spent and price has barely paid, that disagreement *is* the reading.

## Alts

Bitcoin is the clock. Other coins are later. Do not time an altcoin bull off a widget tab.
